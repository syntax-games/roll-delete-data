Delete Your Data — Roll

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Syntax Games ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates Roll ("the Game"). This page explains how you can reset campaign progress in the app.

Roll uses Sign in with Google and saves progress to the cloud. Purchases made with real money are handled by Google Play and stay tied to your Play account.

1. Reset Game Progress (In the App)

Use this if you want to replay the campaign from the beginning. It does not remove your sign-in, purchases, Roll Coins, or unlocked skins.

Steps:

  1. Open Roll
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Reset Progress
  4. Confirm the dialog

What Reset Progress clears:

  • Campaign level completion and unlock state
  • Best star ratings per level
  • Per-level hint unlock progress

What Reset Progress does not change:

  • Your Google sign-in
  • Google Play purchases (Remove Ads, skins bought with real money)
  • Roll Coins and skins you have unlocked
  • Google Play Games achievements (if enabled)

Purchases made through Google Play will restore on the same Google Play account when you open Roll again. Reset Progress only affects campaign progress, not items you own or paid for.

2. Google Play Games Achievements

If you use Google Play Games achievements in Roll, that progress is stored in your Google Play Games profile. Reset Progress in Roll does not remove it. Manage Play Games data using Google's account controls.

For more information, see Google's Play Games data management information .

3. Advertising Data

Roll may display ads through Google AdMob (or related Google advertising services). Advertising data is processed according to Google's policies and your device or consent settings. See Google's Privacy Policy .
