Delete Your Data — Roll

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Syntax Games ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates Roll ("the Game"). This page explains how you can reset game progress or request deletion of your game account and associated save data.

Roll uses Sign in with Google (via Unity Authentication) and stores game progress in Unity Cloud Save. Purchases made with real money are handled by Google Play and are not stored only on our save servers.

1. Reset Game Progress (In the App)

You can reset campaign progress without deleting your sign-in account or your economy progress.

Steps:

  1. Open Roll
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Reset Progress
  4. Confirm the dialog

What Reset Progress clears:

  • Campaign level completion and unlock state
  • Best star ratings per level
  • Per-level hint unlock progress

What Reset Progress does not change:

  • Your Google sign-in / game account
  • Roll Coin balance
  • Collected stage coin pickups (per-level coin IDs)
  • Skins unlocked with Roll Coins
  • Skins or Remove Ads purchased with real money through Google Play
  • Google Play Games achievements (if enabled)

Reset Progress is intended to let you replay the campaign. It does not refund or restore spent Roll Coins and does not allow stage coins to be collected again.

2. Delete Account and Cloud Save

To delete your Roll game account and all cloud save data tied to it, contact us by email. We process verified requests; we do not delete accounts automatically without a request from you.

Steps:

  1. Email inquiry.syntax@gmail.com from the same Google account you use to sign in to Roll
  2. Use the subject line: Roll - Delete Account Request
  3. We will verify the request and delete your data after confirmation

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 7 days. In exceptional cases processing may take up to 30 days.

What we delete after a verified account deletion request:

  • Your Unity Authentication game profile linked to Roll
  • Unity Cloud Save data, including:
    • Level progress and stars
    • Roll Coin balance
    • Per-level collected stage coin IDs
    • Skins unlocked with Roll Coins
    • Equipped cosmetics and settings stored in cloud save
    • Hint unlock progress

What is not removed by us when you delete your game account:

  • Your Google or Google Play account (managed by Google)
  • Google Play purchase records for non-consumable items (for example Remove Ads or skins bought with real money)

Non-consumable purchases remain on your Google Play account. If you install Roll again and sign in with the same Google account and use the same Google Play account on your device, those purchases will restore when the Game checks Google Play Billing.

Consumable Roll Coin packs that were already granted and spent in the Game are not restored after account deletion. A new game profile starts with a fresh economy.

3. Google Play Games Achievements

If you use Google Play Games achievements in Roll, achievement progress may also be stored in your Google Play Games profile. Deleting your Roll cloud save or account through us does not remove Google Play Games data. Manage that data using Google's account and Play Games controls.

For more information, see Google's Play Games data management information .

4. Advertising Data

Roll may display ads through Google AdMob (or related Google advertising services). Advertising data is processed according to Google's policies and your device or consent settings. Deleting your Roll save or account does not delete ad-related data held by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy .
